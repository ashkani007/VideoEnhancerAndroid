# Scene list: *Napoleon: The Rise and Fall of an Emperor* (draft 1, about 17.5 min)

**Shot types:**
- **P** painted plate: generated in ComfyUI on the PC, then picked, upscaled and given metadata.
- **M** animated map: `ghm4.mapshot`, landscape.
- **C** chapter card: renderer typography over a darkened plate.

**Totals:** 52 P, 12 M (14 map designs including shared and optional ones), 10 C; planned 1038 s ≈ 17.3 min.
**Timing:** durations are planned values; after recording, every cut is re-timed to the aligned narration (`ghm4.narrate retime`).

**Motion keys:**
- push = slow push-in; pull = pull-back; drift = lateral move;
- par = matte parallax; smoke / snow / fog / fire / flags / ranks / puffs / blink = renderer effects.
