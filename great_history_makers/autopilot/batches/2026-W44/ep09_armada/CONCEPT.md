# The Armada: Spain Against England (Episode 09)

**Period:** 1588  ·  **Status:** script pending client approval

**Hook:** Spain's great fleet comes to invade England and is undone by fire, guns and weather.

## Narration (send to ElevenLabs unchanged once approved)

1. In 1588, King Philip the Second of Spain sent a great fleet against England.
2. His plan was to carry an invasion army from Flanders across the Channel.
3. About a hundred and thirty ships sailed up the English coast in a vast crescent.
4. Off Calais, the English sent burning fireships into the anchored fleet at night.
5. The Spanish ships cut their cables and scattered.
6. At Gravelines, English guns battered them at close range.
7. Then the wind turned against Spain.
8. Unable to return through the Channel, the Armada sailed around Scotland and Ireland.
9. Storms wrecked dozens of ships, and thousands of men never came home.
10. Queen Elizabeth the First had survived the greatest threat of her reign.
11. England called it a victory sent by God.

## History check

**Established**

- Philip II's Armada (about 130 ships) sailed in 1588 to escort the Duke of Parma's army from Flanders.
- English fireships off Calais (night of 7–8 August, New Style) broke the anchored formation; battle off Gravelines followed.
- Driven into the North Sea, the Armada returned around Scotland and Ireland; storms and wrecks destroyed many ships; thousands died.
- England celebrated the deliverance as providential (thanksgiving service at St Paul's, November 1588).

**Propaganda**

- English and Dutch medals and sermons framed it as divine judgement; Spanish accounts blamed weather.

**Disputed**

- Exact ship losses (often 'about a third to a half', 50–65 ships) and casualty counts; the authenticity of the Tilbury speech text (not quoted).

**Reconstruction**

- Ship models and portraits are stylised; Elizabeth's procession is labelled reconstruction.
