He was born around 1162 as Temüjin, the son of a murdered chieftain.
After his father's death, his family was abandoned on the steppe.
He survived, built alliances, and defeated rival after rival.
In 1206, the Mongol tribes proclaimed him Genghis Khan.
He organized his army in units of ten, a hundred and a thousand, and promoted men for loyalty and skill.
His horsemen broke into northern China, and in 1215 they took the Jin capital.
When the Khwarazmian Empire killed his envoys, he destroyed it.
Cities that resisted were often massacred.
Genghis Khan died in 1227.
His heirs built the largest connected land empire in history.
An empire of terror, and of trade.
