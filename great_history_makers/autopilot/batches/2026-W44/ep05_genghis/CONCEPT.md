# Genghis Khan: From Outcast To Empire (Episode 05)

**Period:** c. 1162–1227  ·  **Status:** script pending client approval

**Hook:** An abandoned boy on the steppe becomes the founder of the largest land empire in history.

## Narration (send to ElevenLabs unchanged once approved)

1. He was born around 1162 as Temüjin, the son of a murdered chieftain.
2. After his father's death, his family was abandoned on the steppe.
3. He survived, built alliances, and defeated rival after rival.
4. In 1206, the Mongol tribes proclaimed him Genghis Khan.
5. He organized his army in units of ten, a hundred and a thousand, and promoted men for loyalty and skill.
6. His horsemen broke into northern China, and in 1215 they took the Jin capital.
7. When the Khwarazmian Empire killed his envoys, he destroyed it.
8. Cities that resisted were often massacred.
9. Genghis Khan died in 1227.
10. His heirs built the largest connected land empire in history.
11. An empire of terror, and of trade.

## History check

**Established**

- Temüjin was born c. 1162 (dates 1155–1167 are proposed); his father Yesügei was poisoned by Tatars.
- Proclaimed Chinggis Khan at a kurultai in 1206; decimal military organisation; promotion of loyal companions (nökörs).
- Campaigns against the Jin; Zhongdu (modern Beijing) fell in 1215.
- The Otrar massacre of a Mongol caravan and the killing of an envoy triggered the Khwarazmian campaign (1219–1221). He died in 1227.

**Propaganda**

- The Secret History of the Mongols is a dynastic epic; Persian chroniclers (Juvaini, Rashid al-Din) wrote under Mongol rule or after devastation; casualty figures in them are often inflated.

**Disputed**

- Birth year; the content and codification of the Yassa; total death tolls of the conquests.

**Reconstruction**

- No contemporary portrait exists; the Yuan-era portrait is posthumous; our depiction is stylised.
