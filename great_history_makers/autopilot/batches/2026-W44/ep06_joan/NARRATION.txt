She was a peasant girl from a village in eastern France.
Around the age of thirteen, Joan said she began to hear the voices of saints.
They told her to help drive the English out of France.
In 1429, at about seventeen, she reached the court of the uncrowned Charles the Seventh.
He gave her armor, a banner, and a place with his army.
At Orléans, the English siege was broken in nine days.
Two months later, Charles was crowned king at Reims.
In 1430, Joan was captured and handed over to the English.
A church court tried her for heresy.
On the thirtieth of May 1431, she was burned at the stake in Rouen.
She was about nineteen.
Twenty-five years later, a new trial overturned her conviction.
