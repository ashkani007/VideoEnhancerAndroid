# Style reference 01 (client, 2026-10-24): napoleon_style_reference_01.png

Painterly editorial illustration with a cinematic poster feel. Only this still arrived; the Instagram video and the
selected frames have not been received.

- **Rendering:** digital gouache-like flat masses with hard-edged shadow shapes and a few soft gradients; visible but
  restrained texture; no outlines except dark accents; faceted, slightly angular folds (cloth, flag, rocks).
- **Light:** low golden-hour sun from the right; warm orange rim on the face, collar and hat edge; cool navy shadows;
  strong value contrast between the foreground figure and the hazy background.
- **Character:** realistic adult proportions (about 7.5 heads), slightly idealised; strong jaw, straight nose, heavy
  brow, dark wavy hair under the bicorne; calm, determined three-quarter gaze; small, precise eyes with a single highlight.
- **Palette:** deep navy, ivory/cream, scarlet red, brass gold, against an apricot-orange and slate-blue sky.
- **Depth:** large foreground figure and props (flag, spyglass, map, rocks); midground cavalry and infantry in silhouette
  with banners; background town, river and bridge dissolving into warm haze.
