## 2. Propaganda
- None shown as fact. "The most feared ruler in Europe" is the narration's rhetorical judgement (see Ep02 file).

## 3. Disputed
- **Size of the 1812 army:** estimates range from about 450,000 to over 600,000. No number is shown on screen.
- **Who set Moscow on fire:** widely attributed to Russian arson under Rostopchin; not stated on screen.

## 4. Reconstruction (labelled or symbolic)
- **Maps:** coastlines are Natural Earth 1:50m (public domain); territory polygons of 1811 are simplified and hand-traced; routes are simplified (Russia: via the Niemen and Smolensk; exile: via Lyon and Saint-Raphaël; return: Golfe-Juan → Grenoble → Lyon → Paris; Saint Helena: via Madeira); coalition arrows are symbolic directions, not troop movements.
- **Illustrations (final, from ComfyUI):** Toulon 1793 battery, the coronation, the close-up, Moscow burning, the retreat, Waterloo, Napoleon at Saint Helena, the Arc de Triomphe and the bicorne on the rock are artistic reconstructions. The bicorne on the rock is symbolic.
