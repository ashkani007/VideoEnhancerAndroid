# History check: Napoleon three-scene test

Narration: the opening of the approved Ep02 narration (unchanged).

## Established
- Napoleon rose from artillery officer (commissioned 1785) to First Consul (1799) and Emperor.
- He crowned himself Emperor of the French on 2 December 1804 (Notre-Dame).
- Victories at Austerlitz (2 Dec 1805), Jena (14 Oct 1806), Friedland (14 Jun 1807) and Wagram (5–6 Jul 1809).
- The empire and its allied and dependent states peaked around 1810–1811.
- The invasion of Russia began in June 1812 (crossing the Niemen); the French entered Moscow in September 1812.

## Propaganda or myth
- The heroic "man of destiny" framing comes largely from Napoleonic imagery; the episode keeps to facts on screen.

## Disputed
- None asserted.
